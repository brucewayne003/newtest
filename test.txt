
Previously, autofocus range was controlled through saved camera preferences and separate camera controls. Apps could not set it directly through OsirisCameraConfig.

This change adds:
- Configurable Near, Far, and unrestricted autofocus ranges.
- Continuous focus targeting at the scanning rectangle’s center or an app-provided point.
- Restoration of the configured focus target and range after tap-to-focus.
- A fix that prevents older cached settings from overwriting the latest saved selection when the scanner restarts.

Existing SDK defaults remain unchanged. Documentation and configuration/focus-policy tests are included.

Validation:
Tested using the Diamond sample app on an iPhone 16 Pro running iOS 27, including checking that the selected autofocus range remains saved after returning to the scanner.
